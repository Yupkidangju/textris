TEXTRIS generated sound-effects provenance (2026-10-08)

Thirty distributed effects were generated locally through Agent Audio using
Stable Audio 3 Medium (TFLite runtime). Thirty-two source generations were retained;
combo and fever_end each received one additional candidate. No model weights or
generation runtime are included in this package.

Requests, generation receipts, original-file hashes and processing measurements
are preserved in assets/audio-production/sfx in the source repository. Source WAVs
are retained in the project's local production archive. Runtime effects were
trimmed to their useful events, faded, level-balanced and encoded as PCM16 WAV.

Model project: https://github.com/Stability-AI/stable-audio-3
Model card: https://huggingface.co/stabilityai/stable-audio-3-medium
Use of model weights is governed by the upstream model license, separately from
the source code. The automated production process did not accept license terms
on behalf of the user. No third-party recording was supplied as a generation input.

Signal and decoding checks do not establish human listening approval or guarantee
that the generated sound follows every acoustic detail of the prompt.
